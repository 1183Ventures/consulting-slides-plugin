Our COO asked whether we should consolidate our four regional warehouses into two. What we know so far:
- freight is 41% of total logistics cost
- the two smallest sites run at 55% and 60% utilization
- their leases expire in 2027 and 2028
- 94% of customers get next-day delivery today

We haven't modeled the savings yet. Draft the slide for Thursday's steerco.
