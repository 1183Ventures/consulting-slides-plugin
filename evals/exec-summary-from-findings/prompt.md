Turn these workstream findings into the executive summary page for Friday's board pre-read.

- Pricing: list prices are 8% below our two main competitors, and discounting adds another 11% of leakage. Reps approve 70% of discounts without review.
- Churn: annual logo churn is 14%, up from 9% two years ago. Exit interviews cite slow support.
- Support: median first response time is 31 hours against a 4-hour SLA. The team is 40% understaffed for current ticket volume.
- Upsell: only 12% of accounts buy a second product. Accounts with two products churn at 4% a year.

Our recommendation is to fix discount governance and support staffing first, then push cross-sell.
